Our Story

Founded with a simple belief — that every person, regardless of ability, deserves the chance to shine. #KINDNESSMATTERS started as a small community group and has grown into a movement that empowers, includes, and celebrates diversity every single day.


Kindness is the bridge between ability and opportunity.

Jeff Rotmeyer, CEO

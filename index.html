Our Story

Founded with a simple belief — that every person, regardless of ability, deserves the chance to shine. #SOMUCHABILITY started as a small community group and has grown into a movement that empowers, includes, and celebrates diversity every single day.


Ability is not about what you can't do — it's about what you CAN do.

Jeff Rotmeyer, CEO
